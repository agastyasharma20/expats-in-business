Foreigners who stay in India beyond a set number of days must **register** with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) or Foreigners Registration Office (FRO) for their area.

## When you must register

- **Any visa:** registration is required if your **aggregate stay in a calendar year, or your continuous stay on a visit, exceeds 180 days**.
- **Student, employment, medical and E-3 visas valid for more than 180 days:** register **within 14 days of arrival**.
- **Business visa:** register when your aggregate stay from January to December, or a continuous stay, exceeds 180 days.
- The Ministry of External Affairs says holders of **multiple-entry visas** register **within 14 days of first arrival** if they intend to stay continuously for more than 180 days.
- **e-Business visa:** the e-Visa page says to register within two weeks after the 180 days have passed. The pages differ, so register early and confirm with the FRRO.
- **Exempt:** OCI cardholders and young children. The Bureau of Immigration says children under 12; the Ministry of External Affairs page says under 16. Use the lower age unless the FRRO tells you otherwise.

## Where

At the FRRO or FRO for your area. Extensions of stay are applied for on **indianfrro.gov.in**.

## Check before you rely on this

India passed a new **Immigration and Foreigners Act in 2025**, with Rules and an Order issued in September 2025. They replace older rules, and details such as who reports a foreigner's arrival (for example a hotel or landlord) now sit there. We could not read those Rules, and the official registration pages above do not state fees or a full document list. Confirm the current steps and fees with the FRRO before you travel.
