## Ways to operate

- An **Indian company**: a joint venture or a wholly owned subsidiary, private or public limited.
- A **limited liability partnership (LLP)**. Foreign investment is allowed under the automatic route in sectors where 100% is allowed and no performance conditions apply.
- For a foreign company: a **liaison office** (to represent the parent), or a **branch or project office**.

## A private limited company

- **At least 2 members and 2 directors** (up to 15 directors). **One director must be resident in India.** (An LLP needs 2 partners, one of them a resident designated partner.) The Invest India guide does not define "resident"; the Companies Act does, so check it before you appoint.
- **Steps:** reserve the name through the MCA's RUN service or on the SPICe+ form; get a **digital signature certificate** for at least one proposed director; apply for each **director identification number (DIN)** through SPICe+; file **SPICe+ (INC-32)** with the e-memorandum (INC-33) and e-articles (INC-34). PAN and TAN are generated automatically, and GST, ESIC and EPFO registrations can be requested through the AGILE form (INC-35).
- **Foreign subscribers:** physical memorandum and articles must be signed, and foreign documents must be **notarised and apostilled or legalised**.
- **Registered office:** you must verify it within **180 days of incorporation**.
- A **foreign company** needs a **resident with a PAN** to receive notices.
- The guide states **no minimum capital**.

## Foreign investment rules

- Investment is by the **automatic route** (no prior approval) or the **government route** (prior approval). Sector limits apply, for example telecom up to 49% by the automatic route. Government-route applications are filed at fifp.gov.in.
- **Equity** must be issued **within 60 days of receiving the money**, or the money is refunded within 15 days after that.
- **Reporting** goes through the RBI's **FIRMS portal**: form **FC-GPR within 30 days** of issuing equity, **LLP-I within 30 days** of receiving consideration, **FC-TRS within 60 days** of a transfer.
- For liaison, branch and project offices, your authorised dealer bank can grant approval, but applicants from some countries and some sectors need the RBI's approval. The rule text we found is from 2016, so read the current RBI directions.

## Start-up recognition

A private limited company, LLP, partnership or cooperative can apply for recognition from the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) if it is up to **10 years** old, has a turnover under **Rs. 200 crore**, and works toward innovation. Apply through nsws.gov.in.

## Check before you rely on this

The MCA and DPIIT pages could not be opened during our check, so the incorporation steps come from the Invest India guide. The Invest India guide also points to the 2020 consolidated FDI policy, so confirm sector rules, including those for investors from countries sharing a land border with India, on the current official pages before you commit money.
