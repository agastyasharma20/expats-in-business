## Residential status for income tax

An individual is **resident** in a tax year if they are in India for **182 days or more** in that year, or for **60 days or more** in the year **and 365 days or more in the 4 years before**. Visiting Indian citizens and people of Indian origin have a longer threshold. Residents who are **not ordinarily resident** are taxed on less foreign income; the Income Tax Department explains the tests.

The new **Income-tax Act, 2025** applies to **tax years starting on or after 1 April 2026** and uses the term "tax year". Older pages still say "previous year" and "assessment year". The residence conditions are the same under both Acts.

## PAN

The **Permanent Account Number** is issued by the Income Tax Department. Foreign individuals who need one for transactions in India use the form for non-citizens on the Department's PAN page. Check there for the current form, documents and fee, because the forms changed with the new Income-tax Rules.

## Aadhaar

Foreign nationals can enrol for Aadhaar if they have **lived in India for 182 days or more in the 12 months before they apply** and hold an **Indian visa or long-term visa (LTV)**, an **OCI card**, or are citizens of **Nepal or Bhutan**.

- You need a **valid Indian visa, OCI card or LTV** together with your valid foreign passport.
- Aadhaar stays valid until the visa expires for visa and LTV holders, and for **10 years** from enrolment for OCI holders and Nepali or Bhutanese citizens.
- An **email address is mandatory** and is checked with a one-time code.

## GST for a foreign business

A foreign business that supplies goods or services in India registers as a **non-resident taxable person**.

- **PAN is not mandatory** for this registration; a passport number or home-country tax ID can be used.
- An **authorised signatory who lives in India and has a PAN** is required.
- Tax must be **deposited in advance**, based on estimated turnover.
- The registration is valid for the period applied for, **at most 90 days**, and can be extended **once by 90 days**.

The turnover thresholds for ordinary GST registration are not confirmed in the official pages we could read. Check them on gst.gov.in.
