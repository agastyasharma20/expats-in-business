Which visa you need depends on what you will do in India. Apply on the official portal, **indianvisaonline.gov.in**. The government does not authorise agents to charge fees for emergency or express visas.

## e-Visa (short business trips)

- Open to nationals of the **175 countries** listed on the portal. Your passport needs **at least six months' validity** when you apply. Pakistani passport holders and holders of diplomatic or official passports are excluded.
- **An e-Visa is not allowed for employment.**
- **e-Business (B1):** valid **one year** from the date of the travel authorisation, multiple entry. Each visit may last **at most 180 days**.
- **e-Business for conferences (B5):** 30 days from arrival.
- Apply **at least 4 days before** you arrive. The fee depends on your nationality (see the fee table) and a 3% bank charge is added. It is not refundable.

## Business visa (B-1 to B-6)

- For people who want to **establish an industrial or business venture or explore possibilities**. Valid **5 years**, multiple entry, extendable. You need proof of the purpose, such as a company letter.
- **Owners and promoters with a majority stake** must use this visa. Holders **cannot take a salary** or remuneration in India.
- An extension needs a turnover of **at least Rs. 1 crore a year** within 4 years.
- Processing takes **a minimum of three working days**. The pages do not state a fee: check with your Indian embassy or visa centre.

## Employment visa (E-1 to E-3)

- For **highly skilled, qualified professionals** engaged by an Indian company or by a foreign company running a project in India.
- The gross salary must be **above Rs. 16.25 lakh a year**. Older government documents quote US$25,000; use the current figure.
- Valid **one year or the contract period**, multiple entry, extendable. You cannot change employer while the visa is valid or work for more than one company at the same time. Code **E-1** covers all employment cases, including intra-company transferees.

## Medical visa (M-1 to M-4)

For treatment at established, recognised or specialised hospitals. Attendants need a Medical Attendant visa, at most two per patient.

## Founders and investors

The official visa lists show **no separate start-up or entrepreneur visa**. Founders use the Business visa. The list also names a **Permanent Residency Status** for foreign investors; we could not confirm its current conditions, so read the Bureau of Immigration list before planning around it.

## Staying and extending

If you plan to stay **more than 180 days**, you must register ([how registration works](/guides/in-foreigner-registration)). Extensions are applied for **inside India**, on indianfrro.gov.in, within the validity of your current visa.
