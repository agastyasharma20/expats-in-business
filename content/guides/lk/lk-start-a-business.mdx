## Company basics

Under the **Companies Act No. 7 of 2007**:

- A company is limited, unlimited or **limited by guarantee**.
- It needs **at least one director** (a public company needs two), who must be 18 or over, and a **company secretary**.
- It must have a **registered office in Sri Lanka**, which can be at a chartered accountant's or attorney's office.
- An **overseas company** that sets up a place of business must register **within one month**, with a list of directors and **persons resident in Sri Lanka authorised to accept service**.
- The Act as read does not state a **minimum capital** or a **resident director** requirement.

The **2025 amendment** allows a company with a **single shareholder**, requires **beneficial owners** to be notified at incorporation and bans bearer shares. We could not confirm the date it takes effect.

Incorporation is done through the Registrar of Companies' **online eROC platform**.

## Rules for foreign investors (Board of Investment)

- **Closed to foreigners:** pawn broking, retail trade with capital under **US$5 million** and coastal fishing.
- **Capped at 40%** unless the BOI approves more: plantation growing and primary processing (tea, rubber, coconut, cocoa, rice, sugar, spices), mining, local timber industries, deep-sea fishing, mass communication, education, freight forwarding, travel agencies and shipping agencies.
- **Need regulator approval:** air transport, coastal shipping, arms, currency, large-scale gem mining, lotteries and others.
- **Land:** freehold transfer is barred to a foreigner or a company with **50% or more** foreign shareholding. Leases run up to **99 years**.

## Board of Investment approval

BOI approval comes first, then incorporation with the Registrar of Companies, then the formal letter of approval. BOI-approved projects must get **prior approval of the draft articles of association**. Fees quoted for a Section 16 application are **US$400 plus US$200** for perusal of the articles; a Section 17 application is US$400, with VAT extra. Minimum investments for BOI incentives include **US$150,000** for IT and BPO and **US$500,000** for tourism and export manufacturing.

## Sole proprietors and partnerships

Register within **one month** of starting; the late penalty is **Rs. 500 a month**. In the Western Province the certificate is issued within **5 working days**. Other provinces run their own systems.

The Registrar's own pages could not be opened during our check, so **incorporation fees and the name-reservation steps are not confirmed here**. Check them on the Registrar's site.
