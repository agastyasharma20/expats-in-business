## The TIN

A **Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)** is mandatory for **resident individuals** aged 18 or over by 31 December 2023, or from the day they turn 18 after 1 January 2024.

**From 1 November 2026** you must show a valid **TIN certificate** to open a bank account, get building-plan approval, register or renew a motor vehicle, register land, register a business, transfer shares or get a credit card. A printout from the IRD's TIN verification page is also accepted.

- **Apply online** through the IRD **e-Services** portal. You need a PIN to use it. The IRD call centre is **1944**.
- **Documents:** an individual uses an **NIC (Sri Lankans) or a valid passport (foreign nationals)**, and a foreigner also needs **proof of a local address**. A company, including a foreign company, needs the **passports of all directors** and a power of attorney for the signatory where it applies.
- **Non-residents:** a 2024 notice said non-residents need not register but are advised to if they plan certain activities. A 2026 notice partly replaces it and concerns residents.

## Who is resident, and what is taxed

You are **resident** if you live in Sri Lanka, or if your presence adds up to **183 days or more in any twelve-month period** that starts or ends in the year. Residents are taxed on income from Sri Lanka and from abroad; non-residents only on Sri Lankan gains and profits.

The **year of assessment** runs **1 April to 31 March**. For **2025/26** the rates for individuals are **6%** on the first Rs. 1,000,000, then **18%**, **24%** and **30%** on each further Rs. 500,000, and **36%** on the rest. **Personal relief is Rs. 1,800,000** for residents and non-resident citizens; the page does not say whether non-citizen non-residents receive it.

Rates and thresholds change often (amending Acts were passed in 2025 and 2026). We found **no tax chart for 2026/27**, so check the IRD site before relying on any rate.

## VAT

You must register if taxable supplies exceed, or are likely to exceed, **LKR 15 million in a quarter** or **LKR 60 million in 12 months**. A proposal to lower the threshold was dropped. The standard rate is **18%**. Voluntary registration is allowed. Register the VAT tax type through e-Services after you have a TIN; VAT needs a request letter, the business registration and director IDs. From **1 July 2026**, **non-residents supplying services through electronic platforms** must register under the same tests.

## Foreign nationals

We found no separate official guidance for foreign nationals: the general resident and non-resident rules apply, and a passport can be used to get a TIN.
