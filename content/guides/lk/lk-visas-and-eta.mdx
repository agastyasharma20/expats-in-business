## Entry

You need an **Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA)** before you arrive, applied for on the official ETA site.

- **Free visa:** since **25 May 2026**, nationals of **40 countries**, including Poland, the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany, India and China, receive a free visa valid for **30 days**, with **double entry** from the date of first arrival. Maldivian nationals receive 90 days.
- **Other nationalities** pay an ETA fee. For SAARC countries it is US$20 for tourist and US$30 for business; for all other countries US$50 for tourist and US$55 for business. Transit is free. Children under 12 are free.
- **No ETA is needed** for holders of a valid resident or permanent resident visa, an entry visa or landing endorsement, or a dual-citizenship certificate.
- **Tourist visas** forbid paid or unpaid work and business. A **business visa** covers meetings, negotiations, conferences and short training, needs an **invitation letter**, and forbids trade, work and self-employment. **Transit** visas are valid 48 hours.

## Extending a stay

Apply **online** or at the head office in Battaramulla. Only applications on the official immigration site count. Tourist extensions cost, for most Western countries, **US$100** (days 31–90), **US$150** (91–180) and **US$200** (181–270). Business extensions cost US$100 (up to 90 days) and US$200 (90–180) and cannot be applied for online. **Overstaying** costs **US$250** for 7–14 days and **US$500** for more than 14 days.

## Living and working there

The immigration pages list **residence visa** categories including **employment, investor, educational, spouse and dependants, medical and digital nomad**.

- **Employment:** the employer applies, with a recommendation from the Board of Investment or the line ministry. Valid one year or as recommended, renewable yearly, and a **Health Protection Plan** certificate is required.
- **Fees:** the standard residence visa is US$200 a year; the **Digital Nomad** visa is US$500; the visa for the **spouse of a Sri Lankan citizen** (marriage over 6 months) is free, first for one year and then in two-year extensions.
- **Resident Guest Scheme:** it only issues extensions to people who already hold it.
- **Permanent residence** is for people of Sri Lankan descent and for spouses married more than **5 years**: US$1,000 or US$400, with a card valid up to 10 years.

## Check before you rely on this

The immigration site prints today's date as its "last updated" date on every page, so that date means nothing. We did not find an official description of how to switch from a visit visa to a residence visa, or an online portal for residence visas. Ask the Department of Immigration and Emigration.
