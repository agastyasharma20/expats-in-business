Polish banks, branches of foreign banks, credit institutions and credit unions (SKOK) must offer a **basic payment account** in Polish złoty.

## Who it is for

Consumers who **do not already have another złoty payment account** in Poland that allows the same transactions.

## What it includes

- Paying money in and withdrawing cash (in any EU member state).
- Payments, including online payments, direct debits, transfers and payments with a **debit** card. Credit cards and other credit products cannot be linked to it.

## Fees

- Running the account for **domestic** payments is **free**.
- The first **5 transfers** a month and the first **5 cash operations** at other providers' machines in Poland are free; the bank may charge for further ones.
- Issuing the debit card is free. Cross-border payments may cost extra.
