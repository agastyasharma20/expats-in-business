## Voluntary insurance

If you are not insured otherwise, you can sign a **voluntary health insurance** agreement with the NFZ.

- **Who:** Polish citizens and foreigners living in Poland who hold a **temporary or permanent residence permit**, and volunteers who meet the same conditions.
- **Where:** apply at your **voivodeship branch** of the NFZ, then sign the agreement you receive.
- **From when:** the agreement **cannot be backdated**. You are entitled to care from the day you submit the application.
- **ZUS:** after signing, register with ZUS and file the monthly **ZUS DRA** declaration for each month you are insured.
- **Extra fee:** if there was a break in your insurance, an **additional fee** may apply; it depends on how long the break was.
