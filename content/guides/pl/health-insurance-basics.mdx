Public healthcare in Poland is run by the National Health Fund (NFZ). You are covered either through **compulsory insurance** (for example through work or your own business) or through **voluntary insurance** that you sign with the NFZ.

## Who is insured automatically

- **Employees**, and people working on a **contract of mandate or an equivalent contract** (agency or services agreement), are insured compulsorily.
- **Sole traders** pay a health contribution to ZUS for themselves. In 2026 the minimum is **432.54 zł a month**; on the general tax scale it is 9% of income, on the flat tax 4.9%, and lump-sum taxpayers pay a fixed amount that depends on annual revenue.
- **Non-EU foreigners working legally** are covered on the same rules as Poles, as long as they are in Poland on a basis such as a work visa, a temporary or permanent residence permit or EU long-term residence.
- Whether a **contract for specific work** (umowa o dzieło) gives compulsory insurance is not stated on the official pages we checked. Ask ZUS or your employer.

## Voluntary insurance

If you are not insured otherwise, you can sign a **voluntary health insurance** agreement with the NFZ.

- **Who:** people who **live in Poland** and have no compulsory insurance and no EU/EFTA cover. Foreigners show a residence card, a decision granting temporary or permanent residence, or a visa with the right to work. Volunteers meeting the same conditions can join too.
- **Where:** apply at your **voivodeship branch** of the NFZ and sign the agreement you receive.
- **From when:** cover starts on the date written in the agreement, **never earlier than the day you submit the application**. It cannot be backdated.
- **ZUS:** after signing, register with ZUS (form ZUS ZZA) and file the monthly **ZUS DRA** declaration for each month you are insured. Payment and declaration are due by the **20th of the following month**.
- **Cost:** 9% of your declared monthly income, but not less than the average enterprise-sector wage of the previous quarter. For October to December 2026 the minimum is **844.77 zł a month**. It changes every quarter, so check the NFZ page. Non-EU students and volunteers pay reduced fixed amounts.
- **Extra fee after a break:** if you were uninsured for **more than 3 months** before signing, an additional fee applies. For a break of 3 months to a year it is **at least 1,877.26 zł**, and it rises with the length of the break. Some foreigners, for example students and holders of residence permits or protection status, are exempt.
