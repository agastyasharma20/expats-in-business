The City of Kraków runs an **Information Point for Foreigners** (Punkt Informacyjny dla Obcokrajowców) together with the **Multicultural Centre** (Centrum Wielokulturowe).

## What it helps with

Information on life in Kraków and on formalities, where to get help, places for integration and how to meet other members of the community.

## Where and when

- **Address:** ul. Zabłocie 20, Kraków, in the Social and Economic Innovation Cluster building (building "A").
- **Opening hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00.
- **Phone:** +48 887 201 598
- **Email:** punkt@open.krakow.pl
