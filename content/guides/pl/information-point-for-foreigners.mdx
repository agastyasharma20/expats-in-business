The **Information Point for Foreigners** (Punkt Informacyjny dla Obcokrajowców) works inside the **Multicultural Centre** (Centrum Wielokulturowe) in Kraków. The City of Kraków commissions and funds it. In 2026–2027 it is run by the **Internationaler Bund Polska Foundation**.

## What it helps with

Residence legalisation, education, administration, official matters and jobs, plus information on life in Kraków, where to get help and places for integration. It also offers legal and psychological consultations, interpreters and assistants for foreign languages. The Point offers free information and advice, and the Centre's offer is **free of charge**.

## Where and when

- **Address:** ul. Zabłocie 20, Kraków, building A, 1st floor (Social and Economic Innovation Cluster).
- **Opening hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00.
- **Phone:** +48 887 201 598
- **Email:** punkt@open.krakow.pl
- **Multicultural Centre:** +48 517 448 487, centrum@open.krakow.pl

## Languages

The City publishes its notices in Polish, English, Ukrainian and Russian and mentions interpreters. We found no current official list of the languages the staff speak, so ask which language you can use when you call or write.
