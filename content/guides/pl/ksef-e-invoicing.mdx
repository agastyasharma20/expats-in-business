KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) is the government system for issuing and receiving structured e-invoices.

## When issuing in KSeF became mandatory

- **1 February 2026** for taxpayers whose sales (including tax) exceeded **200 million zł** in 2024.
- **1 April 2026** for all other taxpayers.
- **Receiving** invoices through KSeF is mandatory from **1 February 2026**.

## The 10,000 zł exception (until the end of 2026)

Until the **end of 2026**, a taxpayer does not have to issue invoices in KSeF if the total value of sales (including tax) documented by such invoices in a month does **not exceed 10,000 zł gross**. The exception ends with 2026.

## What is not covered

- Invoices to **consumers** (individuals not running a business) are outside mandatory KSeF; they may be issued in KSeF voluntarily.
- Taxpayers using the EU **SME exemption** (art. 113a(1) of the VAT Act) cannot issue those invoices in KSeF voluntarily.
