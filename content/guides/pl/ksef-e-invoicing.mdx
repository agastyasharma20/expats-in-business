KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) is the government system for issuing and receiving structured e-invoices.

## When issuing in KSeF became mandatory

- **1 February 2026** for taxpayers whose sales (including tax) exceeded **200 million zł** in 2024.
- **1 April 2026** for all other taxpayers.
- **Receiving** invoices through KSeF is mandatory from **1 February 2026**. Buyers such as consumers and foreign entities receive the invoice in the way agreed with them.

## The 10,000 zł exception ends with 2026

Until **31 December 2026**, a taxpayer does not have to issue invoices in KSeF if the total sales (including tax) documented by such invoices in a month do **not exceed 10,000 zł gross**.

From **1 January 2027**, all invoices must be issued in KSeF **whatever their value**, and buyers who pay by split payment must quote the KSeF number. A bill before the Sejm would postpone the penalties to the end of 2027. It is not law yet and, according to the Ministry, it does not change the date.

## What is not covered

- Invoices to **consumers** (individuals not running a business) are outside mandatory KSeF; they may be issued in KSeF voluntarily.
- Taxpayers using the **SME procedure** (art. 113a(1) of the VAT Act) cannot issue those invoices in KSeF voluntarily.

## Getting access as a sole trader

- You need a **NIP** (tax identification number). You do **not** have to register or report anything to the tax office: as a sole trader you get the owner permissions for your own NIP automatically.
- You log in with your NIP as the context, using one of: **trusted profile, mObywatel, online banking or e-ID card** (through the national node), a **qualified electronic signature**, a **KSeF certificate** issued after your first login, or a **token**, which is available only until **31 December 2026**.
- A free tool for issuing and viewing invoices is the **Taxpayer's KSeF Application** (Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF) at ap.ksef.mf.gov.pl.
- To give your **accountant** access, grant the permission electronically in the application or in your invoicing software. You can grant it to the whole accounting office, which then chooses its own staff.

## If you do not have a PESEL

A trusted profile needs a **PESEL** ([how to get one](/guides/pesel)). A **qualified electronic signature** can be used without a PESEL or NIP, including a foreign one, but you must first report its fingerprint to the tax office on form ZAW-FA (on paper, through the e-Urząd Skarbowy form or through e-Doręczenia). Whether a foreign passport alone, or a foreign eID, is enough to log in is not stated by the official sources we checked.
