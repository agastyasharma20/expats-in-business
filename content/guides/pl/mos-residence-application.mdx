MOS (Moduł Obsługi Spraw) is the government portal for residence applications.

## What must be filed online

Since **27 April 2026**, applications for **temporary residence**, **permanent residence** and **EU long-term resident** status can be filed **only electronically** in MOS. Some application types are still filed on paper under the previous rules.

Paper applications that reached a voivodeship office after 26 April 2026 are not considered, and the postmark date does not count.

## What you need

- You log in to MOS **personally** through login.gov.pl.
- You sign the application with a [**trusted profile**](/guides/trusted-profile), a **qualified electronic signature** or a **personal signature** (podpis osobisty).

## Not everything is online

Only **submitting the application** is fully online. You still go to the office in person to give fingerprints and a signature sample and to show your original passport.

## Fees

- Access to MOS is **free**.
- The stamp duty for the application is **340 zł to 640 zł**, depending on the permit type, plus **100 zł** for issuing the residence card.
- You cannot pay through MOS.
