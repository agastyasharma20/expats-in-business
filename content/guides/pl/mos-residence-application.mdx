MOS (Moduł Obsługi Spraw) is the government portal for residence applications.

## What must be filed online

Since **27 April 2026**, applications for **temporary residence**, **permanent residence** and **EU long-term resident** status can be filed **only electronically** in MOS. Paper applications that reached a voivodeship office after 26 April 2026 are not considered, and the postmark date does not count.

## What is still filed on paper

Some applications are still filed on paper under the previous rules:

- Temporary residence for an **intra-company transfer**, and for **long-term mobility** of a manager, specialist or trainee in an intra-company transfer.
- **Family reunification** when the applicant is **outside Poland**.
- Family members of **Polish, EU or UK citizens** (Withdrawal Agreement, art. 10) and people with family life under the ECHR with such citizens, when the applicant is outside Poland.
- **Replacing a residence card.**

## What you need

- You log in to MOS **personally** through login.gov.pl.
- You sign the application with a [**trusted profile**](/guides/trusted-profile), a **qualified electronic signature** or a **personal signature** (podpis osobisty).

## Not everything is online

Only **submitting the application** is fully online. You still go to the office in person to give fingerprints and a signature sample and to show your original passport. The date of that visit comes from the voivode's office, not from MOS.

## Fees

- Access to MOS is **free**.
- **Stamp duty:** 340 zł for most temporary residence permits, **440 zł** for temporary residence and work (including highly qualified work), **640 zł** for permanent residence and EU long-term resident status. The Office for Foreigners states the range as 340 zł to 640 zł; the voivodeship fee table also lists 170 zł for seasonal work.
- **100 zł** for issuing or replacing the residence card (50 zł for people entitled to a reduction, such as some students and minors).
- You cannot pay through MOS. The card fee goes to the voivodeship office account, separately from the stamp duty.
