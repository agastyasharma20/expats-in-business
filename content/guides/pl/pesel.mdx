PESEL is Poland's personal identification number. Many other steps, such as a [trusted profile](/guides/trusted-profile), need it.

## Two ways to get it

- **Automatically when you register your residence.** You get a PESEL number automatically if you register your residence in Poland for a stay of over 30 days.
- **By application.** If you cannot register your residence but need a PESEL number, apply at any municipality (gmina) office.

## Applying

- Bring the application form (download it or collect it at the office) and a document that confirms your identity and the data you enter in the form.
- As a foreigner you must submit the application **in person**. This does not apply to children born in Poland.
- The service is **free of charge**. The official accepts your application immediately, and you receive a notice once the number is assigned.
