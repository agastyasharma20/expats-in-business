The trusted profile (profil zaufany) is a **free** electronic identification tool. It lets you confirm your identity online and sign documents electronically, for example in PUE ZUS, the Internet Patient Account (IKP) and e-Urząd Skarbowy. It is also one of the ways to sign a residence application in [MOS](/guides/mos-residence-application).

## Who can set one up

- You need a **PESEL number** ([how to get one](/guides/pesel)).
- You must be at least **13 years old**.

## Ways to confirm it

- **Online banking**, if your bank (or another identity provider) offers it; the list is limited.
- **In person at a confirmation point**, also abroad. A residence card is accepted as an identity document there.
- **Video call with an official**, which requires a valid Polish ID card or passport.

## How long it lasts

A trusted profile is valid for **3 years** from confirmation and can be extended for another 3 years at any time.
