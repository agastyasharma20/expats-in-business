The trusted profile (profil zaufany) is a **free** electronic identification tool. It lets you confirm your identity online and sign documents electronically. It is one of the ways to sign a residence application in [MOS](/guides/mos-residence-application).

## Who can set one up

- You need a **PESEL number** ([how to get one](/guides/pesel)).
- You must be at least **13 years old**.

## Ways to confirm it

- **Online banking**, if your bank offers it; the list of banks is limited.
- **In person at a confirmation point**, also abroad. Bring an identity document that confirms who you are unambiguously; the official examples are an identity card, a passport and a **residence card**.
- **Video call with an official**, which needs a **Polish** identity card or a **Polish** passport (a Polish passport counts only if you once held a Polish identity card issued after 31 December 2000). **A foreign passport is not accepted** for the video call, so this route is closed to most foreigners.
- Also listed: the Polish **e-ID card** (e-dowód) and a **qualified certificate**.

If you use a foreign phone number, contact the helpline first so the number can be verified (+48 42 253 54 50).

## Approving actions

Logging in and approving actions now use the **mObywatel app**, which replaced SMS codes as the authorisation method. Logging in through a bank works only if the bank is your authorisation method.

## How long it lasts

A trusted profile is valid for **3 years** from confirmation and can be extended for another 3 years at any time.
