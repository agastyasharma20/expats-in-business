ZUS is Poland's Social Insurance Institution. As a sole trader you pay your own contributions.

## Ulga na start (start-up relief)

- **What it is:** for **six full months** from the start of your business you do not pay social insurance contributions (pension, disability, sickness and accident), the Labour Fund or the Solidarity Fund.
- **Health contribution:** the relief **does not** cover health insurance. During the relief, the health contribution is the only one you pay for yourself.
- **Counting the months:** if you start on the 1st of a month, that month counts; if you start mid-month, it does not.
- **Who qualifies:** you start a business for the first time, or again at least 60 months after you last suspended or closed one, **and** you do not work for a former employer where you were employed in the current or previous calendar year doing the same kind of work.

## After the relief: reduced contributions

Reduced (preferential) contributions are available for the **following 24 full months**. In 2026:

- **Preferential ZUS:** **456.18 zł** a month (calculated from 1,441.80 zł, 30% of the minimum wage), plus health.
- **Full contributions:** **1,926.76 zł** a month (calculated from 5,652.00 zł), plus health.
- **Mały ZUS Plus** (for up to 36 months in a 60-month period): **456.18 zł to 1,788.29 zł** a month, based on last year's income. You qualify if last year's revenue was at most 120,000 zł and the business ran at least 60 days that year.

Amounts for 2027 had not been officially published when this guide was checked. Look at zus.pl in January.

## Health contribution in 2026

On the general tax scale it is 9% of income, on the flat tax 4.9%, and the minimum is **432.54 zł** a month. Lump-sum taxpayers pay 498.35 zł, 830.58 zł or 1,495.04 zł depending on revenue. The minimum was calculated from 75% of the minimum wage in 2025 and from 100% in 2026. The health contribution is settled once a year.

## Registering and filing

- **Register with ZUS within 7 days** of starting, unless you already registered through CEIDG. Use ZUS ZUA to be insured for social and health insurance, or ZUS ZZA if only for health insurance (for example during the start-up relief, with a code starting 05 40).
- For preferential contributions file ZUA with a code starting 05 7. After the 24 months, re-register within 7 days for standard contributions (code 05 10 xx).
- **Every month** file the **ZUS DRA** declaration, even if you pay only for yourself and even during a relief. File and pay by the **20th of the following month**. If that day is a Sunday or a public holiday, the deadline moves to the next working day.
