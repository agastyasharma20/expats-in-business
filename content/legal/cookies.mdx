## Essential cookies

These cookies make the site work or remember a choice you made. They do not need your consent.

| Cookie        | Purpose                                         | Lasts    |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `eib-chapter` | Remembers the chapter you picked in the menu    | 1 year   |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Remembers the language you chose                | Session  |

More essential cookies will be added with sign-in (to keep you signed in) and with the cookie banner (to remember your choice).

## Optional cookies

The platform does not use analytics or advertising cookies today. If we add any, they will only be set after you accept them in the cookie banner, and "Reject all" will be as easy as "Accept all".
