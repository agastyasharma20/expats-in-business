## Why we use it

- **To provide the service you sign up for** (account, events, connections).
- **With your consent** for optional features: marketing emails, matching, "Who I met" and showing your photo to other attendees. You can withdraw consent at any time in your settings.
- **Legitimate interests** such as keeping the platform secure and preventing abuse.

## AI concierge

The concierge answers questions from published guides. Your questions are sent to an AI provider **without** your name, email or other personal data.

## Your rights

Under the [General Data Protection Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj), you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. You will be able to download your data and delete your account from your settings.

You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, the [President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO)](https://uodo.gov.pl/en).
