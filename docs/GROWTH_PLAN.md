# EiB growth plan (draft for the founders)

Status: proposal by the build team, 2026-11-13. No numbers in this document are EiB facts; targets are
suggestions to be set by EiB. What the platform already supports is marked **(built)**.

## 1. Growth loop

Newcomer arrives → finds EiB (search, guides, a friend, a festival) → comes to a session → meets people
(Who I met, connections) → gets value (roadmap, concierge, providers) → invites others, volunteers,
becomes an ambassador → opens the next city. Every step below strengthens one link of this loop.

## 2. Acquisition

- **Guides as the front door (built).** Official-source guides in en/pl/uk rank for the questions every
  newcomer searches (residence permit, PESEL, NFZ, tax). Add one guide a month per country hub.
- **Festivals and Cultoura (built).** Big cultural events are the most shareable moment. Each festival
  has social cards, a spotlight across the site and group tickets; ask attendees to join at the gate
  (QR check-in already creates the "Who I met" link).
- **Referrals.** Next step: a personal invite link per member, with a badge after three newcomers attend.
- **Partners.** Universities' international offices, relocation firms, coworking spaces and embassies:
  each gets a co-branded landing page and an events feed (needs EiB's confirmed partners).

## 3. Activation and retention

- First session within 14 days of sign-up: the onboarding and roadmap reminders already nudge this **(built)**.
- Coffee Roulette, Bubbles and the community feed keep members active between sessions **(built)**.
- Track: sign-up → first check-in conversion and 90-day return rate in the command centre.

## 4. Expansion: city by city

1. **Demand signal (built):** members vote for their city on `/chapters`.
2. **People (built):** country and city ambassadors apply on `/ambassadors`; admins review on `/admin/ambassadors`.
3. **Launch kit:** when a city passes the vote threshold EiB sets, a chapter is created as "launching",
   gets its landmark page, a lead and a first session. India/Indore show this flow with demo data.
4. **Playbook:** the same formats (Business Connect, Virtual Connect, workshops) everywhere, so a new
   lead does not start from zero.

## 5. Community-powered team

- **Volunteers (built):** festival shifts and ongoing roles on `/volunteer`.
- **Careers (built):** internships and, when funded, paid roles on `/careers`.
- Recognition: badges, a yearly volunteer thank-you, LinkedIn-ready certificates for ambassadors.

## 6. Revenue (to confirm with EiB)

Membership stays free. Possible income, all already modelled in the platform with placeholder prices:
paid tickets for large events and festivals, business listings and provider plans, corporate packages
(relocation welcome for new hires), sponsorships with sponsor reports. Prices and sponsors are waiting
for EiB's real figures.

## 7. What we need from EiB

Confirmed vote threshold for launching a city; real partners and sponsors (with permission to show
logos); real festival dates; ambassador benefits EiB can promise; any paid roles and their terms.
